Before You Follow & Do Not Interact

First and foremost: What is a BYF/DNI?

A very chronically-online thing.

Real answer: It's a list of boundaries you set, warning new-comers of what you will and won't tolerate. Of course, this is not something that can be stricly enforced unless someone is directly contacting you (which then means you're able to block them).

I like keeping a DNI because it sets an expectation of what my values are and what type of person I am. It also lets others know what I am not keen on interacting with (but this is not black-and-white, and exceptions are often made for smaller things such as fandom discourse). At this point, though, I have to know and trust you.

I am a very forgiving person, but there are some philosophies I am unflinching on. In terms of what is the "basic DNI criteria" (e.g. being prejudiced/a bigot), I do not turn a blind eye as easily.
