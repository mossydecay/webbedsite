Meet the Webmaster!

Guh. What do I even say about myself.

Uh… Hi!

I've already introduced myself above, but I'm Luca! Nerdy History major (w/ a minor in Spanish), always way too passionate about their interests, and over-ambitious in everything I do.

Made this site as a way to chuck all my fave things in one place, but it's certainly not the first time I've undertaken such a project. I've been on the web for way too long and am immensely comfortable navigating it, despite how soul-sucking it can be at times. Hence, indie web. I am chronically online, unfortunately, so I'm hoping having my own site will help direct my energy to someplace that is't as… exposed, y'know?
